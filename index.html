01 / About

Bio.

My background spans signal processing, machine learning and deep learning, with experience in 3D reconstruction and document understanding. I hold a Master’s in Computer Vision (UAB) and a Bachelor’s in Telecommunications Engineering (UPC), and have worked in industry and research in Barcelona and Lausanne, including LTS5 at EPFL.

Today, I’m a researcher in Eurecat’s Digital Health Unit and a PhD candidate with the AIBA group at Universitat de Barcelona, supervised by Prof. Petia Radeva and Dr. Vicent Ribas. My current work explores generative models and visual synthesis, applied to medical imaging.

03 / Publications

Selected publications.

Accepted — WACV 2027

MUMINS: Metadata-conditioned Uncertainty-aware Medical Image Next-state Synthesis

Anna Oliveras, Roger Marí, Rafael Redondo, Oriol Guardià-Olivella, Cynthia Ifeyinwa Ugwu, Ana Tost, Bhalaji Nagarajan, Carolina Migliorelli, Vicent Ribas, Petia Radeva

IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision

A diffusion framework that synthesizes a future 3D scan from a baseline scan and a target time interval, producing a voxel-wise uncertainty map in the same sampling process. Evaluated on lung CT and brain MRI.
