Slice 01 — Profile

About

I hold a Master's degree in Computer Vision (UAB) and a Bachelor's in Telecommunications Engineering (UPC), and have built my expertise in signal processing, machine learning and deep learning through industry roles and research internships across Barcelona, Lausanne and beyond. I'm currently a PhD researcher at the Universitat de Barcelona and Eurecat, supervised by Prof. Petia Radeva and Dr. Vicent Ribas, focused on generative models for synthetic medical imaging — mainly CT and MRI. What draws me to the field is the chance to put AI to work on problems that matter clinically, alongside people who care about getting the details right.
