Profile

PhD researcher in generative AI for medical imaging at the University of Barcelona and Eurecat, supervised by Prof. Petia Radeva and Dr. Vicent Ribas. I develop diffusion models that synthesise 3D medical images (CT and MRI) and forecast how a patient’s scan will evolve over time, with work published in Scientific Reports and accepted at WACV 2027. Background in signal processing, machine learning and deep learning from an MSc in Computer Vision, a BSc in Telecommunications Engineering, industry experience in medical computer vision, and research internships in Barcelona and Lausanne.
